# Code of Conduct

Nova Terminal is built by people with different backgrounds, experience and opinions. We want taking part to be a good experience for everyone, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Explain your reasons and stay open to being wrong.
- Give feedback that is specific and helps the work move forward, and accept feedback the same way.
- Respect people's time: search before asking, and keep discussions on topic.

## What isn't acceptable

- Insults, personal attacks, and sexist, racist or otherwise discriminatory remarks
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private, including unwanted sexual attention
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or sustained disruption
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Where this applies

In all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainer, [Umesh Bakotiya](https://github.com/umeshbakotiyadev), privately. Reports are handled in confidence, and nobody will face retaliation for reporting in good faith.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will look into every report and respond in proportion. That can mean a private warning, removing comments or code, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project. Maintainers who don't follow this code in good faith may face the same consequences.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
